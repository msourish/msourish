## FOI Surround Monitor

I wanted to hear a six-channel DaVinci Resolve mix on the Sony 5.1 system I already owned. FOI Surround Monitor takes the processed Fairlight bus, gives me a meter and level control for each channel, and sends live Dolby Digital 5.1 to the receiver. I tested the speaker assignments on a Mac mini M4 with a Sony HT-S20R connected through an HDMI-to-optical extractor.

[Project, installation guide, and speaker check →](https://github.com/msourish/FOI-Surround-Monitor#readme)
